The **opening** is roughly the first 10–15 moves. You do not need to memorize lines — just remember **three golden rules**.

## 1. Take the center

The four squares **d4, e4, d5, e5** are the heart of the board. Central pieces control more squares and can switch to either side quickly.

## 2. Develop your minor pieces

Get your **knights and bishops** off the back rank as soon as possible. Move each piece only once in the opening.

## 3. Castle early

Put your king in safety and connect your rooks.
