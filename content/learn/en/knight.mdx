The knight has the **most unusual move**: an **L-shape**.

## How it moves

The knight goes **two squares in one direction** (horizontally or vertically), then **one square to the side**. It is the **only piece that can jump** over others.

<Board fen="4k3/8/8/8/3N4/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1" show="d4">In the center, a knight has 8 moves.</Board>

<Callout type="tip">
Every knight move **changes square color**: light to dark, or dark to light.
</Callout>

## A knight on the rim is dim

On the edge or in a corner, a knight has very few moves. That is why knights usually head **toward the center**.

<Board fen="4k3/8/8/8/8/8/8/N3K3 w - - 0 1" show="a1">A knight in the a1 corner has only 2 moves.</Board>
