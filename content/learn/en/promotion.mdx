When a pawn reaches the **last rank** (the 8th for White, the 1st for Black), it is **promoted**: it turns into a queen, rook, bishop or knight.

<MoveSequence fen="8/4P1k1/8/8/8/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1" moves="e8=Q">The e7 pawn steps to e8 and becomes a queen.</MoveSequence>

## Which piece?

Almost always a **queen** — the strongest piece. You can have **two queens** or more on the board. Occasionally players pick a **knight** to give check right away, or another piece to avoid **stalemate**.

<Callout type="rule">
A pawn **may not** become a king, and it cannot stay a pawn.
</Callout>

The c7 pawn can **capture and promote at the same time**. Take the black queen and promote to a queen!
</Exercise>

<Callout type="tip">
In the endgame, a pawn about to promote is as strong as a major piece. Escort it with your king and rook!
</Callout>
