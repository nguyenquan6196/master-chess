The **endgame** is the final phase, when only a few pieces are left. The rules of thumb change completely!

## 1. Activate your king

In the opening the king hides behind pawns. In the endgame, with few pieces left, the king is **safe to fight** — bring it to the center.

<Board fen="8/8/8/4k3/8/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1" arrows="e1e3">White should march the king toward the center right away.</Board>

## 2. Push passed pawns

A **passed pawn** has no enemy pawns in front of it or on the neighboring files. It is a promotion candidate — push it!

<Board fen="8/8/8/8/3P4/8/8/k3K3 w - - 0 1" arrows="d4d8">The d4 pawn is passed: the road to d8 is wide open.</Board>

## 3. Mate with a rook

With king and rook you can mate the lone king: drive it to the **edge of the board**, place your king opposite it, and check along the edge with the rook.

<Board fen="R5k1/8/6K1/8/8/8/8/8 b - - 0 1">The a8 rook checks along the back rank and the g6 king covers f7, g7 and h7: checkmate!</Board>

<Callout type="tip">
In endgames, counting moves matters: whoever promotes first usually wins.
</Callout>
