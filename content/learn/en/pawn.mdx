Each side has **8 pawns**. They are small but important: they form the skeleton of your position.

## How it moves

- A pawn moves **straight forward one square**.
- On its **very first move**, a pawn may move **one or two squares**.
- A pawn **never moves backward**.

<Board fen="4k3/8/8/8/8/8/4P3/4K3 w - - 0 1" show="e2">The pawn on e2 can go to e3 or e4.</Board>

## How it captures

Pawns move straight but **capture diagonally** — one square diagonally forward. If something stands right in front of a pawn, it is stuck.

<Board fen="4k3/8/8/3p1p2/4P3/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1" show="e4">The e4 pawn can go to e5, or capture on d5 or f5.</Board>

<Callout type="warning">
Pawns can never come back, so think before pushing one: the squares behind it lose its protection.
</Callout>

<Callout type="fun">
Pawns have two special tricks: **en passant** and **promotion** on the last rank. You will learn both in the Rules chapter.
</Callout>
