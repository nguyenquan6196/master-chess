Not all pieces are equally strong. Players use **points** to compare them:

| Piece | Value |
|---|---|
| Pawn | 1 |
| Knight | 3 |
| Bishop | 3 |
| Rook | 5 |
| Queen | 9 |
| King | priceless |

## Trading

When capturing or trading, add up the points. Giving a knight (3) for a rook (5) is **good**; giving a queen (9) for a rook (5) is **bad**.

Your knight can take a rook or a pawn. Choose the **best capture**.
</Exercise>

## Hanging pieces

A piece that is attacked and **not defended** is "hanging". Always check whether your opponent has a hanging piece — and whether you do!

<Exercise kind="make-move" fen="4k3/8/8/2b5/8/8/8/2R1K3 w - - 0 1" answers="c1c5" explain="Rxc5: nothing defends the black bishop, so it is yours for free.">
The black bishop is hanging. **Take it**.
</Exercise>

<Callout type="tip">
When you are **ahead in material**, trade pieces to simplify. When you are **behind**, avoid trades.
</Callout>

<Callout type="rule">
Points are only a guide. A very active piece can be worth much more than the table says.
</Callout>
