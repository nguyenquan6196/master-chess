**Castling** is the only move where **two pieces move at once**: the king and a rook. It tucks the king away and brings the rook into play.

## How to castle

- **Kingside** (O-O): the king moves 2 squares toward the near rook (e1 → g1), and the rook jumps next to it (h1 → f1).
- **Queenside** (O-O-O): the king moves 2 squares toward the far rook (e1 → c1), and the rook goes from a1 to d1.

<Callout type="tip">
In this app, just **drag the king two squares** toward the rook — the rook moves by itself.
</Callout>

## The four conditions

<Callout type="rule">
1. Neither the king nor that rook **has moved before**.
2. The squares between them **are empty**.
3. The king is **not in check**.
4. The king **does not pass through or land on** an attacked square.
</Callout>
