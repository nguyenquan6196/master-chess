Each side has **2 bishops**: one on light squares, one on dark squares.

## How it moves

A bishop moves **diagonally**, any number of squares, as long as **nothing is in the way**. Because it only moves diagonally, a bishop **stays on one color all game**.

<Board fen="4k3/8/8/8/3B4/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1" show="d4">A bishop on d4 controls two long diagonals.</Board>

## Blocked, and capturing

A bishop cannot pass through pieces. It can **capture** an enemy piece in its path, but must stop before one of its own.

<Callout type="tip">
Bishops love **open diagonals**. A bishop stuck behind its own pawns is called a "bad bishop".
