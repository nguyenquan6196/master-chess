Not every game has a winner. A game is **drawn** in these cases:

| Case | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stalemate | The side to move is not in check but has no legal move |
| Threefold repetition | The same position occurs 3 times (same side to move) |
| Fifty-move rule | 50 moves by each side with no capture and no pawn move |
| Insufficient material | Neither side has enough pieces left to checkmate |
| Agreement | Both players agree to a draw |

## Threefold repetition

<MoveSequence moves="Nf3 Nf6 Ng1 Ng8 Nf3 Nf6 Ng1 Ng8">Both sides move their knights out and back. The starting position appears for the 3rd time — draw.</MoveSequence>

## Insufficient material

King vs king, king + bishop vs king, or king + knight vs king **cannot checkmate** — the game is drawn at once.

<Board fen="8/8/4k3/8/8/2B5/4K3/8 w - - 0 1">A lone bishop cannot checkmate. Draw!</Board>

<Callout type="tip">
When you are worse, a draw is a good result! Look for repetitions or trade everything off.
</Callout>
