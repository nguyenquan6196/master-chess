Chess is played on a **square board of 64 squares**, alternating light and dark. One player has the **White** pieces, the other the **Black** pieces — 16 each.

<Board>The starting position: White at the bottom, Black at the top.</Board>

## Ranks and files

- The board has **8 ranks** (rows), numbered **1 to 8** from White's side.
- And **8 files** (columns), lettered **a to h** from left to right, seen from White's side.
- There are also **diagonals** — lines of same-colored squares touching at the corners.

<Board fen="8/8/8/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1" squares="a1 b2 c3 d4 e5 f6 g7 h8">A long diagonal: from a1 to h8.</Board>

## Setting up the board

<Callout type="rule">
The square in each player's **bottom-right corner** must be **light**. Remember: _"light on the right"_.
</Callout>

The second rank holds 8 pawns; the first rank, from left to right: rook, knight, bishop, queen, king, bishop, knight, rook. **The queen goes on her own color** — the white queen on light d1, the black queen on dark d8.

<Callout type="tip">
**White always moves first.** Then the players take turns, one move at a time.
</Callout>

## The goal of the game

The goal isn't to capture every piece, but to **checkmate** the enemy king — attack it so it has no way to escape. You'll learn this properly in the Rules chapter.

<Callout type="fun">
There are more possible chess games than atoms in the observable universe!
</Callout>
