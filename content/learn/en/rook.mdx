Rooks start in the four corners. After the queen, they are the strongest pieces.

## How it moves

A rook moves **straight along ranks and files**, any number of squares if the path is clear.

## Rooks love open files

An **open file** has no pawns on it. A rook on an open file can charge straight into the enemy camp.

<Exercise kind="make-move" fen="3qk3/8/8/8/8/8/8/3RK3 w - - 0 1" answers="d1d8" explain="Rxd8+! Even if the king takes back, you traded a rook (5) for a queen (9).">
The d-file is open. Use your rook to **capture the black queen**.
</Exercise>

<Callout type="tip">
Rooks are very strong in the endgame. A great habit: put your rook **behind a passed pawn** (a pawn with no enemy pawns in its way).
</Callout>
