## Check

When a piece attacks the king, the king is in **check**. The side in check **must get out of check on the very next move**, in one of three ways:

1. **Move** — step the king to a safe square.
2. **Block** — put a piece between the king and the attacker.
3. **Capture** — take the checking piece.

## Checkmate

If the king is in check and there is **no way out** — it cannot move, block or capture — it is **checkmate** and the game is over. The side that delivered mate wins.

<MoveSequence moves="f3 e5 g4 Qh4#">The fastest possible checkmate: after just 2 moves, White loses for opening the way for the black queen.</MoveSequence>

## Stalemate

If it is a side's turn, their king is **not in check**, but they have **no legal moves**, the game is a **draw** — called **stalemate**.

<Board fen="7k/5Q2/6K1/8/8/8/8/8 b - - 0 1">Black to move: the king is not in check, but every square is covered. Stalemate!</Board>

<Callout type="warning">
When you are winning big, be careful not to stalemate your opponent. Always leave the enemy king at least one move until you deliver mate.
</Callout>
