The king is the **most important piece**: lose it and you lose the game. In fact the king is never captured — the game ends as soon as it is **checkmated**.

## How it moves

The king moves **one square in any direction**: horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

<Board fen="8/8/8/8/3K4/8/8/7k w - - 0 1" show="d4">A king on d4 has 8 squares to go to.</Board>

## Never into danger

The king **may not move onto a square attacked by an enemy piece**. That is why the two kings can never stand next to each other.

## Check

When a piece attacks the king, the king is in **check**. The side in check **must deal with it immediately**. You will learn how in the next chapter.

<Callout type="tip">
Early on, keep your king safe behind its pawns. In the endgame, with few pieces left, the king becomes a strong fighter!
</Callout>
