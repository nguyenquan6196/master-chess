The queen is the **most powerful piece** on the board. Each side has just one.

## How it moves

The queen combines the **rook and the bishop**: along ranks, files or diagonals — any distance if the path is clear.

## Handle with care

The queen is so valuable (about 9 points) that you do not want to trade it for a smaller piece.

<Callout type="warning">
Do not bring the queen out too early. Your opponent will develop pieces while chasing your queen around, and you will lose time.
</Callout>

<Callout type="rule">
At the start, **the queen goes on her own color**: the white queen on d1 (light), the black queen on d8 (dark).
</Callout>
